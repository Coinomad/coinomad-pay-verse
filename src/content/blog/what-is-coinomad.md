# What is Coinomad?

**Coinomad is stablecoin settlement infrastructure.** We give treasury teams and the platforms that move money a faster, cheaper way to settle across borders — payment links, virtual business accounts, and an enterprise API, running on seven markets in both directions.

That sentence carries a lot. This post unpacks it.

## The layer money actually runs on

Most people never think about settlement. They see a payment go out and a balance update. But underneath every cross-border transfer is a chain of correspondent banks, cut-off times, and intermediaries, each taking a slice and adding a day of float.

Settlement is the unglamorous layer that decides whether a payment lands the same day or three days later — and what it costs along the way.

Coinomad exists because that layer is overdue for an upgrade. Stablecoins settle in minutes, not days, on rails that are open 24/7 and programmable. Our job is to make those rails safe, compliant, and usable by a finance team without them having to become a crypto team.

## What we build

Three products, one settlement layer.

**Stablecoin payment links and widgets.** A customer pays in USDC, USDT, or a bank transfer into a virtual account. The business settles to its bank or holds the stablecoin. One link turns an invoice or a storefront into a working checkout — no code required.

**Virtual business accounts.** Dashboard-issued accounts in supported regions for cross-border payments, plus high-volume stablecoin-to-fiat conversion in both directions. Collect where your customers are, settle where your treasury lives.

**Enterprise APIs.** The same virtual fiat and stablecoin wallet capabilities, extended to your own platform. Fintechs, iGaming operators, and exchanges embed settlement directly into their product. KYC and KYB are handled for you, including conversion between assets.

## Seven markets, both directions

"Both directions" is the part that matters. Plenty of rails move money into a market or out of it. We operate seven markets — **NGN · USD · EUR · GBP · CAD · MXN · BRL** — and value can flow in *and* out of each, converted on the way.

That means a treasury can collect in Lagos, convert to USD, and pay a contractor in Mexico City on the same rails, without opening seven local banking relationships.

## Why stablecoins change the arithmetic

Strip away the noise and a stablecoin is a simple thing: a digital dollar or euro that settles near-instantly on open infrastructure. That gives treasury teams three things correspondent banking has never offered:

- **Speed.** Minutes, not the three-plus days of a typical cross-border wire.
- **Cost.** No correspondent chain means no chain of intermediary fees — and far less spread.
- **Control.** Settlements are programmable, auditable, and reversible only when you say so.

We pair that with the rails that reach the last mile — local clearing through ACH, SEPA, PIX, SPEI, Interac, and NGN — so the fast settlement actually lands where the recipient can use it.

## Built for the people who move money

Our customers are treasury teams, B2B operators, payroll platforms, stores and merchants, and iGaming businesses. What they share is a simple problem: money crosses borders constantly, and every leg of that journey costs time and margin.

Coinomad is also built with compliance at the centre, not bolted on. We're a participant in the Central Bank of Nigeria's Sandbox Cohort 2, operating as a regulated Virtual Asset Service Provider.
