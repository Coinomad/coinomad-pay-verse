Any asset → any other asset

Three ways to draw the settlement core.

Not "stablecoin to fiat" — but every currency and stablecoin flowing into a central infrastructure and back out as any other. EUR→NGN, NGN→CAD, BRL→USDC. Three institutional framings of the same idea; pick the one that matches the brand you're building.

A

The Clearing Hub

A central settlement core with every asset ringed around it. Value enters the core from any side and exits as any other — a national clearinghouse in motion.
